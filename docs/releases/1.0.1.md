**English** · [中文](#中文)

Vesqen 1.0.1 fixes queue editing with shuffle on, keeps your favorites when you rename or move files in an added folder, and corrects how albums are grouped and lists are sorted.

## Upgrading

Open Settings → Updates and tap Check for updates, or download `Vesqen-1.0.1.apk` below and install it over 1.0.0 or any beta from beta.2 on. Your library, playlists, favorites and settings stay. Vesqen 1.0.1 needs Android 9.0 or later.

## What's fixed

- **Shuffle.** With shuffle on, Play next now plays the song right after the current one and Add to queue plays it last. Moving a song up or down in the queue changes the order songs play in, and the queue lists that order. Turning shuffle on starts from the song that's playing, so none of the others are skipped. Turning shuffle off goes back to the list order, with the songs you added in place.
- **Renamed and moved files.** In a folder added with Add music folder, a song you rename, or move to another folder inside it, keeps its favorite, play count and playlist places. A song that leaves the folder keeps them too, out of sight, until it is back.
- **Reopening after the end.** After an album has finished, Vesqen starts it from the beginning, also when you swiped the notification away. If you paused just before the end of a song, it continues that song where you left it, also with Repeat one on. Songs shorter than a second are no longer skipped.
- **Albums.** Albums with the same name by different artists stay apart when the files have no album artist tag, and an album split into disc folders such as "CD1" or "Album (Disc 2)" stays one album. Songs sorted by album and an artist's album count follow the same grouping.
- **Sorting.** Albums, artists, folders and genres now sort like Songs: by first letter, Chinese names by the first letter of their pinyin, and names starting with a digit or symbol last. Inside a folder, files keep their usual order, so "01 Intro" still comes before "Bonus Track".
- **Buttons.** Text buttons in dialogs, the queue and the pages are now full size for touch and for TalkBack.
- **Bluetooth codec.** On Android 13 and 14, codec numbers 5 and 6 are shown as an unnamed codec with their sample rate and bit depth. Some phone makers use these numbers for their own codecs, so Vesqen no longer calls them LC3 or Opus there. Android 15 and later name the codec themselves, as before.

## Install

- Requirements: Android 9.0 or later. Strict USB output needs Android 14 or later and a compatible USB DAC.
- Download `Vesqen-1.0.1.apk` below. SHA-256: `(发布时填写)`
- Application signing certificate SHA-256: `74:3E:96:FC:B7:1D:C5:81:88:49:68:19:A0:01:A2:7C:D8:8D:90:91:62:C5:AE:92:9C:87:BF:A2:9A:B8:62:93`. Check it with `apksigner verify --print-certs`.
- The [Support page](https://vesqen.sumirenokai.com/support/#install-title) on the Vesqen website explains step by step how to install the APK.

## Known limitations

- No phone and DAC combination has been verified bit-perfect yet, so no `BIT-PERFECT VERIFIED` record exists.
- Strict USB output hasn't been confirmed with a real DAC yet. Many phones don't offer Android's bit-perfect path, even on Android 14 and later. None of the Android 14+ phones we tested (vivo and iQOO, on Android 15 and 16) offer it, so strict mode stops there and tells you why. System output still plays through USB DACs and speakers as usual.
- Android 13 and earlier don't provide the official bit-perfect mixer API, so strict USB output isn't available there.
- Even on phones that do offer the bit-perfect path, strict USB output currently stops for most files: Vesqen hands Android floating-point audio, and USB DACs' bit-perfect formats are integer. It stops and says why instead of playing something that isn't bit-perfect. This is being fixed together with Vesqen's own USB output, planned for 2.0.
- In strict USB mode, there is a short gap between tracks, even on gapless albums.
- With shuffle on, the shuffled order isn't kept after Android stops Vesqen in the background: when you come back, the queue is shuffled again.
- Bluetooth is never bit-perfect. The Chain page can say that Vesqen's part left the audio unchanged; the phone still re-encodes it for Bluetooth.
- After Android stops Vesqen in the background, the Play button in the notification shade and on the lock screen can't restart it on Android 13 and later. Headphone buttons may not work either: the vivo (Android 15) and Honor (Android 9) phones we tested pass them only to music apps on a list built into the phone's system, and Vesqen isn't on it. Open Vesqen to continue where you left off.
- On vivo phones, Vesqen's playback controls show on the lock screen only after you turn on Lock screen notifications for Vesqen in the phone's notification settings.
- Some phone makers' systems pause music on their own. For example, on our vivo test phone, the system paused Vesqen about three minutes after the media volume was turned all the way down with the volume keys.
- Vesqen doesn't move its data to a new phone. Favorites, playlists and settings stay on the old phone.
- Vesqen hasn't been tested on foldables, and its smoothness at high refresh rates hasn't been measured. TalkBack was checked on an Android 16 emulator, not yet on a phone.

The current list, with what is being fixed before 2.0 and what 2.0 is planned to bring, is in [issue #163](https://github.com/Sumire-no-kai/Vesqen/issues/163) and on the [website](https://vesqen.sumirenokai.com/status/).

## 中文

Vesqen 1.0.1 修正了随机播放时的队列编辑，在已添加的文件夹里改名或移动文件后收藏不再丢失，也修正了专辑的分组和列表的排序。

### 升级

打开"设置 → 更新"点"检查更新"，或者下载下面的 `Vesqen-1.0.1.apk`，直接覆盖安装 1.0.0 或 beta.2 及以后的测试版。曲库、歌单、收藏和设置都会保留。Vesqen 1.0.1 需要 Android 9.0 及以上。

### 修正了什么

- **随机播放。** 随机播放打开时，"下一首播放"会排在当前歌曲之后，"添加到队列"会排在最后。在队列里上移、下移歌曲，改的就是播放顺序，队列列表显示的也是这个顺序。打开随机播放时从正在播放的歌曲开始，其余歌曲一首也不会漏。关掉随机播放后回到列表原来的顺序，加进来的歌曲留在各自的位置。
- **改名和移动的文件。** 用"添加音乐文件夹"加入的文件夹里，把歌曲文件改名，或者移到文件夹里的其他位置，收藏、播放次数和歌单位置都会保留。歌曲移出这个文件夹时，这些数据也会先保留下来，文件放回来后恢复。
- **播完后重新打开。** 专辑播完后，即使划掉了通知，重新打开 Vesqen 也会从头开始。如果在歌曲快结束时暂停，会从暂停的位置继续播放这首歌，单曲循环时也一样。短于一秒的歌曲不再被跳过。
- **专辑。** 文件没有专辑艺术家标签时，不同艺术家的同名专辑会分开显示；分在"CD1"或"专辑名 (Disc 2)"这类分碟文件夹里的专辑仍是一张。按专辑排序的歌曲列表和艺术家的专辑数也按同样的方式分组。
- **排序。** 专辑、艺术家、文件夹和流派列表现在和"歌曲"一样排序：按首字母分组，中文名称按拼音首字母，以数字或符号开头的排在最后。文件夹里的文件仍按原来的顺序，"01 Intro"会排在"Bonus Track"前面。
- **按钮。** 对话框、队列和各页面里的文字按钮，点击区域都改成标准尺寸，TalkBack 读到的也是这个尺寸。
- **蓝牙编码。** 在 Android 13 和 14 上，编号 5 和 6 的编码会显示为未命名的编码，并显示采样率和位深。部分手机厂商把这两个编号用在自己的编码上，所以 Vesqen 在这些系统上不再把它们写成 LC3 或 Opus。Android 15 及以上由系统给出编码名称，和以前一样。

### 安装

- 系统要求：Android 9.0 及以上。严格 USB 输出需要 Android 14 及以上和兼容的 USB DAC。
- 下载下面的 `Vesqen-1.0.1.apk`。SHA-256：`(发布时填写)`
- 应用签名证书 SHA-256：`74:3E:96:FC:B7:1D:C5:81:88:49:68:19:A0:01:A2:7C:D8:8D:90:91:62:C5:AE:92:9C:87:BF:A2:9A:B8:62:93`，可以用 `apksigner verify --print-certs` 核对。
- Vesqen 官网的[支持页](https://vesqen.sumirenokai.com/zh/support/#install-title)上有安装 APK 的分步说明。

### 已知限制

- 目前还没有任何手机和 DAC 的组合通过 bit-perfect 验证，所以还没有 `BIT-PERFECT VERIFIED` 记录。
- 严格 USB 输出还没有在真实的 DAC 上确认过。很多手机即使是 Android 14 及以上，也没有开放 Android 的 bit-perfect 通路；我们测过的 Android 14 及以上手机（vivo 和 iQOO，Android 15 和 16）都没有，严格模式在这些手机上会停止播放并说明原因。用系统输出时，USB DAC 和 USB 音箱照常可以播放。
- Android 13 及以下没有官方的 bit-perfect 混音接口，所以不能使用严格 USB 输出。
- 即使手机开放了 bit-perfect 通路，严格 USB 输出目前遇到大多数文件也会停止：Vesqen 交给 Android 的是浮点音频，而 USB DAC 的 bit-perfect 格式是整数。它会停止播放并说明原因，不会播放一个其实不是 bit-perfect 的结果。这个问题会和计划在 2.0 推出的 Vesqen 自研 USB 输出一起解决。
- 严格 USB 模式下切歌时会有短暂的间隔，无缝专辑也一样。
- 随机播放时，Vesqen 在后台被系统停止后，随机顺序不会保留，回来时队列会重新随机。
- 蓝牙不可能 bit-perfect。链路页可以说明 Vesqen 这一段没有改动音频，但手机仍会为蓝牙重新编码。
- Vesqen 在后台被系统停止后，在 Android 13 及以上，通知栏和锁屏上的播放按钮不能让它恢复。耳机按键也不一定可以：我们测过的 vivo（Android 15）和 Honor（Android 9）手机只把耳机按键交给系统内置名单里的音乐应用，Vesqen 不在名单里。打开 Vesqen 就能从原来的位置继续。
- vivo 手机上，要在系统的通知设置里为 Vesqen 打开"锁屏通知"，锁屏上才会显示 Vesqen 的播放控件。
- 有些手机厂商的系统会自己暂停音乐。例如在我们的 vivo 测试机上，用音量键把媒体音量调到 0 约三分钟后，系统暂停了 Vesqen。
- Vesqen 不会把数据迁移到新手机，收藏、歌单和设置会留在原来的手机上。
- 还没在折叠屏上测过，高刷新率下的流畅度也还没有测量。TalkBack 在 Android 16 模拟器上检查过，还没在真机上测。
