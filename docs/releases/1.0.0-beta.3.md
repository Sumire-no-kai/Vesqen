**English** · [中文](#中文)

The third public beta of Vesqen, an offline music player for Android. It plays the music stored on your phone, needs no account, and shows the path each track takes from the file to your headphones.

## Upgrading from beta.2

Open Settings → Updates and tap Check for updates, or wait for the daily check. Vesqen downloads the update only when you ask, checks its SHA-256 and signature, and Android asks you to confirm the install. Your library, playlists, favorites and settings stay. You can also download `Vesqen-1.0.0-beta.3.apk` below and install it over beta.2.

## What's new

- **Pro features.** Settings now has a Pro features page. Strict USB output is a Pro feature and stays free until version 2.0; the first time you turn it on, Vesqen explains this once.
- **Crash reports you choose to send.** If Vesqen closed unexpectedly or stopped responding, it asks once the next time you open it whether to send a report. The report shows the error type and where in the code it happened, never file paths or song titles, and you see all of it before choosing to upload, share or email it.
- **A smoother first run.** The usage statistics question fits on the first screen. An empty library now leads with Allow access to device music, with adding a folder as the second choice. After a scan, a list that was at the top stays at the top.
- **Fixes:** a Play pressed while strict USB output was being prepared is no longer lost; device report buttons keep their labels whole at large text sizes; the day's update check no longer gets skipped when no usage statistics were sent; the Paused note on usage statistics explains that it can stay for up to a day after a pause ends.

## Install

- Requirements: Android 8.0 or later. Strict USB output needs Android 14 or later and a compatible USB DAC.
- Download `Vesqen-1.0.0-beta.3.apk` below. SHA-256: `(发布时填写)`
- Application signing certificate SHA-256: `74:3E:96:FC:B7:1D:C5:81:88:49:68:19:A0:01:A2:7C:D8:8D:90:91:62:C5:AE:92:9C:87:BF:A2:9A:B8:62:93`. Check it with `apksigner verify --print-certs`.

## Known limitations

- No phone and DAC combination has been verified bit-perfect yet, so no `BIT-PERFECT VERIFIED` record exists.
- Strict USB output hasn't been confirmed with a real DAC yet. Many phones don't offer Android's bit-perfect path, even on Android 14 and later. None of the Android 14+ phones we tested (vivo and iQOO, on Android 15 and 16) offer it, so strict mode stops there and tells you why. System output still plays through USB DACs and speakers as usual.
- Android 13 and earlier don't provide the official bit-perfect mixer API, so strict USB output isn't available there.
- Bluetooth is never bit-perfect. The Chain page can say that Vesqen's part left the audio unchanged; the phone still re-encodes it for Bluetooth.
- After Android stops Vesqen in the background, the Play button in the notification shade and on the lock screen can't restart it on Android 13 and later. Headphone buttons may not work either: the vivo (Android 15) and Honor (Android 9) phones we tested pass them only to music apps on a list built into the phone's system, and Vesqen isn't on it. Open Vesqen to continue where you left off. On phones where headphone Play does restart Vesqen, a car stereo or phone that sends Play when it connects will start playback too.
- Vesqen doesn't move its data to a new phone. Favorites, playlists and settings stay on the old phone.
- Long listening: an 8-hour session passed on an Android 9 phone with wired headphones. Long sessions with the screen off, on newer Android versions, and with interruptions such as calls or unplugged headphones are still being tested.
- Foldables, high refresh rates and complete TalkBack coverage haven't been fully tested yet.

## Privacy

Playback and your library still never use the network. As in beta.2, Vesqen uses it only for usage statistics, device reports you choose to upload, and update checks. Crash details stay on your phone, at most 3 crashes for 7 days, and leave it only inside a report you choose to send. The [privacy policy](https://vesqen.sumirenokai.com/privacy/) lists what each one sends and how long it is kept.

## Price

The betas and version 1.x are free. Library, playback and Chain are always free. Pro features such as strict USB output stay free until version 2.0; from then on, you can try them free for 7 days and then unlock them with a one-time purchase, with no subscription and no ads. Not every phone offers Android's official bit-perfect path, so we're building Vesqen's own USB output for those phones. It's planned for 2.0, but we can't promise it yet.

## Feedback

Email vesqen@sumirenokai.com or [open an issue](https://github.com/Sumire-no-kai/Vesqen/issues/new/choose). A device report (Settings → Privacy & data → Export device report) tells us your phone, Android version and output path, so you don't have to type them.

**Test the Google Play version.** We are preparing Vesqen for Google Play and need testers with a Google account who can keep the test version installed for 14 days. To join, email vesqen@sumirenokai.com with the Google account address you use on Google Play.

## 中文

Vesqen 的第三个公开测试版。Vesqen 是一款 Android 离线音乐播放器，播放你手机里的音乐，不用注册账号，还会写明每首歌从文件到耳机经过的路径。

### 从 beta.2 升级

打开“设置 → 更新”点“检查更新”，或者等每天的自动检查。只有你同意时才下载，下载后核对 SHA-256 和签名，安装时由 Android 请你确认。曲库、歌单、收藏和设置都会保留。也可以下载下面的 `Vesqen-1.0.0-beta.3.apk`，直接覆盖安装 beta.2。

### 这个版本有什么

- **高级功能。** 设置里多了“高级功能”页。严格 USB 输出属于高级功能，2.0 之前免费；第一次打开它时，Vesqen 会说明一次。
- **由你决定是否发送的崩溃报告。** Vesqen 意外退出或无响应后，下次打开时会询问一次是否发送报告。报告包含错误类型和出错的代码位置，不含文件路径或歌名；你会先看到全文，再决定上传、分享还是发邮件。
- **第一次使用更顺手。** 使用统计的问题在第一屏就能回答完。曲库为空时，主按钮改为“允许访问设备音乐”，“添加音乐文件夹”是第二个选择。扫描完成后，原本停在顶部的列表仍停在顶部。
- **修复：** 严格 USB 输出准备期间按下的播放不再丢失；大字号下，设备报告的按钮文字不再被拆开；当天没有发送使用统计时，自动检查更新不再被跳过；使用统计的“已暂停”说明里写明，暂停结束后它最多还会显示一天。

### 安装

- 系统要求：Android 8.0 及以上。严格 USB 输出需要 Android 14 及以上和兼容的 USB DAC。
- 下载下面的 `Vesqen-1.0.0-beta.3.apk`。SHA-256：`(发布时填写)`
- 应用签名证书 SHA-256：`74:3E:96:FC:B7:1D:C5:81:88:49:68:19:A0:01:A2:7C:D8:8D:90:91:62:C5:AE:92:9C:87:BF:A2:9A:B8:62:93`，可以用 `apksigner verify --print-certs` 核对。

### 已知限制

- 目前还没有任何手机和 DAC 的组合通过 bit-perfect 验证，所以还没有 `BIT-PERFECT VERIFIED` 记录。
- 严格 USB 输出还没有在真实的 DAC 上确认过。很多手机即使是 Android 14 及以上，也没有开放 Android 的 bit-perfect 通路；我们测过的 Android 14 及以上手机（vivo 和 iQOO，Android 15 和 16）都没有，严格模式在这些手机上会停止播放并说明原因。用系统输出时，USB DAC 和 USB 音箱照常可以播放。
- Android 13 及以下没有官方的 bit-perfect 混音接口，所以不能使用严格 USB 输出。
- 蓝牙不可能 bit-perfect。链路页可以说明 Vesqen 这一段没有改动音频，但手机仍会为蓝牙重新编码。
- Vesqen 在后台被系统停止后，在 Android 13 及以上，通知栏和锁屏上的播放按钮不能让它恢复。耳机按键也不一定可以：我们测过的 vivo（Android 15）和 Honor（Android 9）手机只把耳机按键交给系统内置名单里的音乐应用，Vesqen 不在名单里。打开 Vesqen 就能从原来的位置继续。在耳机播放键能让 Vesqen 恢复的手机上，连接时会自己发送“播放”的车机或手机也会让它开始播放。
- Vesqen 不会把数据迁移到新手机，收藏、歌单和设置会留在原来的手机上。
- 长时间播放：一台 Android 9 手机接有线耳机连续播放 8 小时通过。关屏播放、较新 Android 版本上的长时间播放，以及来电、拔耳机等中断情况还在测试中。
- 折叠屏、高刷新率和完整的 TalkBack 读屏体验也还没有测完。

### 隐私

播放和曲库仍然从不使用网络。和 beta.2 一样，Vesqen 只在三件事上使用网络：使用统计、你自己选择上传的设备报告，以及检查更新。崩溃记录只保存在你的手机上，最多 3 次、7 天，只有放进你选择发送的报告里才会离开手机。[隐私政策](https://vesqen.sumirenokai.com/zh/privacy/)写明了每一项发送什么、保留多久。

### 价格

测试版和 1.x 免费。曲库、播放和链路一直免费。严格 USB 输出等高级功能在 2.0 之前免费；2.0 起可以免费试用 7 天，之后一次性买断，不做订阅，也没有广告。不是所有手机都开放了 Android 官方的 bit-perfect 通道，我们正在开发 Vesqen 自己的 USB 输出，计划在 2.0 推出，让这些手机也能严格直出，目前还不能保证。
