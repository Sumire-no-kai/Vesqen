**English** · [中文](#中文)

Vesqen 1.0 is the first stable version of Vesqen, an offline music player for Android. It plays the music stored on your phone, needs no account, and shows the path each track takes from the file to your headphones.

## Upgrading from a beta

Open Settings → Updates and tap Check for updates. Vesqen also checks by itself when you open it, at most once a day. It downloads the update only when you ask, checks its SHA-256 and signature, and Android asks you to confirm the install. Your library, playlists, favorites and settings stay. You can also download `Vesqen-1.0.0.apk` below and install it over any beta.

Vesqen 1.0 needs Android 9.0 or later. On Android 8.0 and 8.1, the updater doesn't offer it, and beta.3 keeps working as it is.

## What's new since beta.3

- **Japanese and Traditional Chinese.** Vesqen now speaks English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Japanese. On Android 13 and later you can pick its language in the system's app language settings. The Japanese text hasn't been reviewed by a native speaker yet, so corrections are welcome.
- **Bluetooth codec on the Chain page.** On Android 13 and later, after you allow Nearby devices (Vesqen asks only when you tap Show Bluetooth codec), the Chain page shows the codec Android reports, with its sample rate and bit depth, and for LDAC the estimated bitrate of the quality setting. Android sends this when a connection is set up, so headphones that were already connected before Vesqen started, or before you allowed it, may need to reconnect.
- **A clearer Chain page.** The key readings are pinned above the path and easier to read. Lossy sources are judged against what the decoder actually outputs, and the Bluetooth route shows on Android 11 to 13 too. On Android 11 and later, the route now follows changes while Vesqen runs: before, it kept naming the first output it saw, for example Bluetooth headphones after Bluetooth was turned off. WAV and AIFF files played without a decoder now say so, and route and decoder types are written out instead of shown as internal codes.
- **Faster.** Vesqen ships a Baseline Profile, so once Android has compiled it, Vesqen starts sooner and scrolls more smoothly. Google Play compiles it when it installs the app; for the APK, Android does it during its next background optimization, usually while the phone is idle and charging. Fast scrolling through a large library drops far fewer frames, and moving between Settings and the Chain page is quicker.
- **Safer library data.** Favorites, play counts and playlist entries stay with their files when Android rebuilds its media library. Removing a folder or deleting a playlist now asks first.
- **Library.** Albums, artists, folders and genres are listed in reading order, with Chinese names by pinyin, as in Songs. Sorting artists, genres or playlists by Artist now sorts them by name. Giving a playlist a name that's already used tells you so. When you reopen Vesqen after an album has finished, it starts from the beginning instead of at the end of the last song.
- **Mini player.** A flatter capsule shape, full-size touch targets for its buttons, and titles that are no longer cut off. On a narrow phone with very large text, it leaves out Previous so the title has room.
- **Large text and TalkBack.** Folders are listed by their own name, with the path on the second line on Android 10 and later. At large text sizes, buttons, track numbers and the liner notes no longer break mid-word. TalkBack says which song is playing or paused, and can move songs up and down while you edit an order.
- **Navigation.** Closing Now returns to the tab you opened it from. Settings keeps its place when you come back from a page opened from it. Tapping the tab you're on no longer resets the page.
- **Privacy fixes.** If your phone's time zone is in a region where we ask before sending usage statistics, Vesqen now asks. Device reports from self-built Android versions no longer include the builder's login name.
- **Support development** (this GitHub version only). About has a Support development link, and now and then Vesqen asks whether you'd like to support it, saying what the support is for. It's voluntary, unlocks nothing, and you can turn the question off.

## Known limitations

- No phone and DAC combination has been verified bit-perfect yet, so no `BIT-PERFECT VERIFIED` record exists.
- Strict USB output hasn't been confirmed with a real DAC yet. Many phones don't offer Android's bit-perfect path, even on Android 14 and later. None of the Android 14+ phones we tested (vivo and iQOO, on Android 15 and 16) offer it, so strict mode stops there and tells you why. System output still plays through USB DACs and speakers as usual.
- Android 13 and earlier don't provide the official bit-perfect mixer API, so strict USB output isn't available there.
- Even on phones that do offer the bit-perfect path, strict USB output currently stops for most files: Vesqen hands Android floating-point audio, and USB DACs' bit-perfect formats are integer. It stops and says why instead of playing something that isn't bit-perfect. This is being fixed together with Vesqen's own USB output, planned for 2.0.
- In strict USB mode, there is a short gap between tracks, even on gapless albums.
- With shuffle on, Play next and Add to queue put the song at a random point in the shuffled order, and the queue list shows the order before shuffling. A fix is planned before 2.0.
- In a folder added with Add music folder, renaming a song file or moving it to another folder inside it makes Vesqen treat it as a new song at the next scan, so its favorite, play count and playlist places are lost. A fix is planned before 2.0.
- Bluetooth is never bit-perfect. The Chain page can say that Vesqen's part left the audio unchanged; the phone still re-encodes it for Bluetooth.
- After Android stops Vesqen in the background, the Play button in the notification shade and on the lock screen can't restart it on Android 13 and later. Headphone buttons may not work either: the vivo (Android 15) and Honor (Android 9) phones we tested pass them only to music apps on a list built into the phone's system, and Vesqen isn't on it. Open Vesqen to continue where you left off.
- On vivo phones, Vesqen's playback controls show on the lock screen only after you turn on Lock screen notifications for Vesqen in the phone's notification settings. Our vivo test phone had it off for Vesqen.
- Some phone makers' systems pause music on their own. For example, on our vivo test phone, the system paused Vesqen about three minutes after the media volume was turned all the way down with the volume keys.
- Long listening and interruptions: 8 hours with wired headphones on Android 9, 4 hours with the screen off on Android 15 (a 24-bit/96 kHz track, 3 of them on battery), incoming calls on an Android 16 emulator, unplugging wired headphones, and disconnecting Bluetooth headphones on Android 15 all passed: playback pauses when the headphones go away and doesn’t start again by itself when they come back.
- Vesqen doesn't move its data to a new phone. Favorites, playlists and settings stay on the old phone.
- Vesqen hasn't been tested on foldables, and its smoothness at high refresh rates hasn't been measured: our measurements on the 120 Hz test phone ran at 60 Hz. TalkBack was checked on an Android 16 emulator, not yet on a phone, and phone makers' own screen readers haven't been tested.

The current list, with what is being fixed before 2.0 and what 2.0 is planned to bring, is in [issue #163](https://github.com/Sumire-no-kai/Vesqen/issues/163) and on the [website](https://vesqen.sumirenokai.com/status/).

## Privacy

Playback and your library still never use the network. Vesqen uses it only for usage statistics, device reports you choose to upload, and update checks. The Support development link only opens a web page in your browser. The [privacy policy](https://vesqen.sumirenokai.com/privacy/), updated on October 20, lists what each one sends and how long it is kept.

## Price

Version 1.x is free. Library, playback and Chain are always free. Pro features such as strict USB output stay free until version 2.0; from then on, you can try them free for 7 days and then unlock them with a one-time purchase, with no subscription and no ads. Not every phone offers Android's official bit-perfect path, so we're building Vesqen's own USB output for those phones. It's planned for 2.0, but we can't promise it yet.

## Google Play

Vesqen is in a closed test on Google Play. Once Google approves it for everyone, the same version 1.0 will be available there. Both versions are signed with the same key, so you can later install the GitHub version over the Play version and keep your data.

## Feedback

Email vesqen@sumirenokai.com or [open an issue](https://github.com/Sumire-no-kai/Vesqen/issues/new/choose). A device report (Settings → Privacy & data → Export device report) tells us your phone, Android version and output path, so you don't have to type them.

**Support development.** If Vesqen is useful to you, you can [buy us a coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/edward_lee). Support goes into Vesqen's own USB output engine and into making playback right on more phones and DACs. It's voluntary and doesn't unlock anything.

Vesqen 1.0 是第一个正式版。Vesqen 是一款 Android 离线音乐播放器，播放你手机里的音乐，不用注册账号，还会写明每首歌从文件到耳机经过的路径。

### 从测试版升级

打开"设置 → 更新"点"检查更新"。Vesqen 也会在你打开它时自己检查，每天最多一次。只有你同意时才下载，下载后核对 SHA-256 和签名，安装时由 Android 请你确认。曲库、歌单、收藏和设置都会保留。也可以下载下面的 `Vesqen-1.0.0.apk`，直接覆盖安装任何一个测试版。

Vesqen 1.0 需要 Android 9.0 及以上。Android 8.0 和 8.1 的手机收不到这次更新，beta.3 可以照常使用。

### 这个版本有什么（相对 beta.3）

- **日语和繁体中文。** 界面现在有英文、简体中文、繁体中文和日语四种语言。Android 13 及以上可以在系统的"应用语言"设置里单独选择 Vesqen 的语言。日语译文还没有经过母语者审读，欢迎指正。
- **链路页显示蓝牙编码。** 在 Android 13 及以上，允许"附近的设备"权限后（只在你点"显示蓝牙编码"时才会询问），链路页会显示系统报告的蓝牙编码、采样率和位深，LDAC 还会显示所选音质档位的估算码率。Android 只在建立连接时发出这些信息，所以在 Vesqen 启动前或允许权限前就已连着的耳机，可能要重新连接一次才会显示。
- **链路页更清楚。** 关键读数置顶在路径上方，更容易看清。有损音源按解码器的实际输出来判断；Android 11 到 13 也会显示蓝牙路径。在 Android 11 及以上，路由现在会跟着变化更新；以前会一直停在 Vesqen 最先读到的输出，例如关掉蓝牙后仍显示蓝牙耳机。WAV 和 AIFF 没有经过解码器时会如实显示；路由类型和解码方式用文字写出，不再显示内部代码。
- **更快。** Vesqen 带上了 Baseline Profile，Android 编译好之后，启动更快、滚动更顺。从 Google Play 安装时会直接编译；APK 要等 Android 下一次后台优化，通常是在手机空闲并充电时。大曲库快速滑动时掉帧少了很多，设置和链路页之间切换也更快。
- **曲库数据更安全。** Android 重建媒体库后，收藏、播放次数和歌单里的歌会跟着原来的文件保留。移除文件夹或删除歌单前会先确认。
- **曲库。** 专辑、艺术家、文件夹和流派按阅读顺序排列，中文按拼音，和“歌曲”一致。艺术家、流派和播放列表按“艺术家”排序时，按各自的名称排。新建或重命名播放列表时，名称已被使用会有提示。专辑播完后重新打开 Vesqen，会从头开始，不再停在最后一首的末尾。
- **迷你播放条。** 外形改成更扁的胶囊，按钮的点击区域加大到标准尺寸，歌名不再被截断。窄屏手机上字体很大时，会收起"上一首"，给歌名留出位置。
- **大字体和 TalkBack。** 文件夹列表先显示文件夹自己的名字，Android 10 及以上把路径放在第二行。大字体下，按钮、曲目编号和链路注记不再从词中间断行。TalkBack 会说出哪首歌正在播放或已暂停；编辑顺序时，也能用 TalkBack 上移、下移歌曲。
- **导航。** 关闭"正在播放"后回到打开它之前的那个页签。从设置打开的页面返回时，设置停在原来的位置。再点一次当前页签不再把页面重置。
- **隐私方面的修正。** 如果手机的时区属于需要先征得同意才发送使用统计的地区，Vesqen 会先询问。自行编译的 Android 系统，设备报告里不再带上编译者的登录名。
- **支持开发**（仅 GitHub 版）。"关于"里有"支持开发"链接；Vesqen 也会偶尔询问你是否愿意支持，并说明支持用在哪里。支持是自愿的，不解锁任何功能，询问也可以关掉。

### 已知限制

- 目前还没有任何手机和 DAC 的组合通过 bit-perfect 验证，所以还没有 `BIT-PERFECT VERIFIED` 记录。
- 严格 USB 输出还没有在真实的 DAC 上确认过。很多手机即使是 Android 14 及以上，也没有开放 Android 的 bit-perfect 通路；我们测过的 Android 14 及以上手机（vivo 和 iQOO，Android 15 和 16）都没有，严格模式在这些手机上会停止播放并说明原因。用系统输出时，USB DAC 和 USB 音箱照常可以播放。
- Android 13 及以下没有官方的 bit-perfect 混音接口，所以不能使用严格 USB 输出。
- 即使手机开放了 bit-perfect 通路，严格 USB 输出目前遇到大多数文件也会停止：Vesqen 交给 Android 的是浮点音频，而 USB DAC 的 bit-perfect 格式是整数。它会停止播放并说明原因，不会播放一个其实不是 bit-perfect 的结果。这个问题会和计划在 2.0 推出的 Vesqen 自研 USB 输出一起解决。
- 严格 USB 模式下切歌时会有短暂的间隔，无缝专辑也一样。
- 随机播放打开时，“下一首播放”和“添加到队列”会把歌曲放到随机顺序里的任意位置，队列列表显示的也是打乱前的顺序。计划在 2.0 之前修复。
- 用“添加音乐文件夹”加入的文件夹里，如果把歌曲文件改名，或者移到这个文件夹里的其他位置，下次扫描时 Vesqen 会把它当成一首新歌，原来的收藏、播放次数和歌单位置会丢失。计划在 2.0 之前修复。
- 蓝牙不可能 bit-perfect。链路页可以说明 Vesqen 这一段没有改动音频，但手机仍会为蓝牙重新编码。
- Vesqen 在后台被系统停止后，在 Android 13 及以上，通知栏和锁屏上的播放按钮不能让它恢复。耳机按键也不一定可以：我们测过的 vivo（Android 15）和 Honor（Android 9）手机只把耳机按键交给系统内置名单里的音乐应用，Vesqen 不在名单里。打开 Vesqen 就能从原来的位置继续。
- vivo 手机上，要在系统的通知设置里为 Vesqen 打开"锁屏通知"，锁屏上才会显示 Vesqen 的播放控件。我们的 vivo 测试机上，Vesqen 的这一项是关着的。
- 有些手机厂商的系统会自己暂停音乐。例如在我们的 vivo 测试机上，用音量键把媒体音量调到 0 约三分钟后，系统暂停了 Vesqen。
- 长时间播放和中断：Android 9 上接有线耳机连续播放 8 小时、Android 15 上关屏播放 4 小时（24 位 / 96 kHz，其中 3 小时用电池）、Android 16 模拟器上的来电中断、拔掉有线耳机，以及 Android 15 上断开蓝牙耳机，都已通过：耳机断开时播放暂停，耳机接回来也不会自己继续播放。
- Vesqen 不会把数据迁移到新手机，收藏、歌单和设置会留在原来的手机上。
- 还没在折叠屏上测过，高刷新率下的流畅度也还没有测量：我们在 120 Hz 测试机上的测量都是在 60 Hz 下做的。TalkBack 在 Android 16 模拟器上检查过，还没在真机上测，手机厂商自带的读屏也没测。

最新的列表，以及 2.0 之前要修的问题和 2.0 的计划，放在 [issue #163](https://github.com/Sumire-no-kai/Vesqen/issues/163) 和[官网](https://vesqen.sumirenokai.com/zh/status/)上。

### 隐私

播放和曲库仍然从不使用网络。Vesqen 只在三件事上使用网络：使用统计、你自己选择上传的设备报告，以及检查更新。"支持开发"链接只是在浏览器里打开一个网页。10 月 20 日更新的[隐私政策](https://vesqen.sumirenokai.com/zh/privacy/)写明了每一项发送什么、保留多久。

1.x 免费。曲库、播放和链路一直免费。严格 USB 输出等高级功能在 2.0 之前免费；2.0 起可以免费试用 7 天，之后一次性买断，不做订阅，也没有广告。不是所有手机都开放了 Android 官方的 bit-perfect 通道，我们正在开发 Vesqen 自己的 USB 输出，计划在 2.0 推出，让这些手机也能严格直出，目前还不能保证。

### Google Play

Vesqen 正在 Google Play 上封闭测试，Google 批准公开上架后，会上架同一个 1.0 版本。两个版本用同一把签名密钥，以后可以用 GitHub 版直接覆盖安装 Play 版，数据会保留。

### 反馈

发邮件到 vesqen@sumirenokai.com，或者[新建 issue](https://github.com/Sumire-no-kai/Vesqen/issues/new/choose)。附上一份设备报告（"设置 → 隐私与数据 → 导出设备报告"），我们就能知道你的手机、Android 版本和输出路径，不用你一项项填写。

**支持开发。** 如果 Vesqen 对你有用，可以[请我们喝杯咖啡](https://buymeacoffee.com/edward_lee)。支持会用在 Vesqen 自研的 USB 输出引擎，以及在更多手机和 DAC 上把播放做对。这是自愿的，不会解锁任何功能。
