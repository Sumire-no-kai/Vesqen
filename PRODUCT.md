# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vesqen serves people who keep and play a local music library on Android, including listeners who value offline access and privacy, lossless-audio enthusiasts, and advanced users who connect USB DACs and want to understand the playback chain. The primary workflow must remain obvious to a first-time listener: grant access, choose a track, and control playback. Technical evidence is available progressively for users who seek it.

## Product Purpose

Vesqen is a lightweight, offline-first local music player that combines an immediate listening experience with an auditable audio path. Success means that a new user can start music without learning audio engineering, while an expert can inspect what the app, Android, and connected hardware can actually prove without inflated claims.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and intimate. Vesqen should feel like a trusted listening instrument: quiet enough to disappear behind the music, exact enough to deserve confidence, and personal rather than clinical. Its voice is concise, factual, and reassuring.

## Anti-references

- Feed-heavy streaming services that prioritize recommendations, social activity, advertising, or engagement loops over the user's own library.
- Direct imitation of Spotify, NetEase Cloud Music, Apple Music, or another recognizable player.
- Neon gaming-audio dashboards, spectrum visualizer spectacle, and dense audiophile telemetry on the default screen.
- Generic glassmorphism, decorative blur, oversized rounded-card grids, purple-gradient dark mode, and animation without a state purpose.
- Generic music notes, headphones, play triangles, vinyl records, waveforms, or checkmarks as the product mark.
- Brand identity that changes unpredictably with Android dynamic color.

## Design Principles

1. **Play first; prove on demand.** The default surface helps the user choose and play music. Metadata, diagnostics, and evidence unfold progressively.
2. **One obvious next action.** Every state—first launch, empty library, paused playback, output change, or failure—makes the primary next step unmistakable.
3. **Truth has hierarchy.** Source facts, observed state, derived conclusions, and unavailable evidence remain visually and verbally distinct.
4. **Quiet surfaces, purposeful signal.** Color, light, blur, and motion indicate focus or state; they never compete with the music or decorate empty space.
5. **Local is a benefit, not a burden.** Privacy and offline behavior are explained clearly without turning ordinary playback into a warning-filled workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The UI targets WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, 48 dp minimum touch targets, complete TalkBack semantics, logical focus order, scalable text, and English plus Simplified Chinese from the first public release. Meaning must never rely on color alone. Every motion pattern requires a reduced-motion alternative, and translucent or blurred surfaces require an opaque high-contrast fallback.
